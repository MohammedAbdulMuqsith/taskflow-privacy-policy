Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 7, 2026 • Application: TaskFlow

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which Mohammed Abdul Muqsith, publishing under OmniCraft ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our"), collects, uses, maintains, and discloses information collected from users ("User", "you") of the TaskFlow mobile application (the "Application") on the Google Play Store. We are committed to transparency and adherence to Google Play Developer Program Policies, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).

Core Privacy Commitment: TaskFlow collects only the minimal data required to deliver core task management and productivity functionality, maintain application security, and prevent software regressions.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in three ways: information you directly provide, information collected automatically during app execution, and technical diagnostic telemetry.

A. Information You Provide to Us

  • Account Information: If you register or sign in (via email or federated authentication such as Google Sign-In), we collect your email address, display name, and unique authentication identifier assigned by our identity provider.
  • User-Generated Content: Tasks, task titles, descriptions, due dates, checklists, priority tags, and organizational metadata entered within the Application.
  • Customer Support Correspondence: If you reach out to us for technical assistance, we retain your email, message content, and any attached logs or screenshots you voluntarily supply.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device and Network Information: Device hardware model, operating system version (Android API level), primary language, screen resolution, and approximate cellular/Wi-Fi network connection state.
  • Identifiers: Pseudonymous installation identifiers, Google Play Services Advertising ID (AAID, if enabled and not reset by user), and Firebase Instance IDs utilized strictly for push notification routing and service initialization.
  • Usage Metrics: Session start/end timestamps, feature engagement metrics (e.g., tasks completed, screen transitions), and system interaction events.

C. Crash and Diagnostic Data

  • Crash Reports: Stack traces, memory state snapshots at the time of an unhandled exception, CPU architecture, device orientation, and battery level at crash time, collected to troubleshoot app crashes.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your data exclusively for legitimate operational, security, and service delivery purposes:

  • To provide, synchronize, and maintain core productivity and task management services across your sessions.
  • To authenticate user sessions, prevent unauthorized account access, and enforce system security.
  • To monitor stability, identify software regressions, debug crashes, and push updates.
  • To deliver relevant push notifications and task reminders scheduled explicitly by you.
  • To comply with statutory legal requirements and enforce our platform terms.

3. Third-Party Service Providers and SDKs

We integrate vetted third-party software development kits (SDKs) to facilitate cloud infrastructure, authentication, and application monitoring. These entities may collect or process pseudonymous device telemetry under their respective privacy notices:

Service Provider Purpose Data Shared / Collected
Google Play Services Core Android framework integrations, security verification, API delivery Device specs, OS version, Google account identifier
Google Firebase Authentication User account login, identity management, access tokens Email, federated auth tokens, user UID
Firebase Firestore / Database Cloud persistence and cross-device synchronization User-generated tasks, lists, and metadata
Firebase Crashlytics & Analytics Real-time crash reporting, error monitoring, engagement telemetry Crash logs, stack traces, app session timing, device IDs

For more information on Google's privacy practices regarding Firebase and Play Services, review Google's Privacy & Terms and Firebase Data Privacy Documentation.

4. Google Play Data Safety Compliance Disclosures

In accordance with Google Play's Data Safety requirements, the following architecture applies:

  • No Third-Party Advertising Sharing: We do not sell your personal data or share user-identifiable data with third-party advertising brokers.
  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the Application, Google Firebase infrastructure, and external APIs are strictly encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher (HTTPS).
  • Independent Data Deletion: Users have an accessible, automated mechanism to request total account and content deletion.

5. Data Retention Policy

We retain your user data and synchronized content only as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide application functionality. Diagnostic crash logs collected via Firebase Crashlytics are automatically retained for 90 days before permanent erasure. If an account is inactive for more than 24 continuous months, we reserve the right to delete all associated cached records and cloud data stores.

6. Account and Data Deletion (User Rights)

You have full ownership of your data. In compliance with Google Play's User Data Policy, you may request the deletion of your account and all associated personal records at any time through either of the following methods:

Method 1: In-App Deletion

If you have an active account, navigate to Settings > Account > Delete Account within the Application. Confirming this action permanently deletes your user profile, tasks, and linked authentication credentials immediately.

Method 2: Manual Web/Email Request

If you cannot access the Application, you can request total data deletion by sending an email with the subject "Account & Data Deletion Request - TaskFlow" to our developer support desk:

official.omnicraft@gmail.com (or direct developer contact at mohammedabdulmuqsith24680@gmail.com)

Please include the primary email address linked to the account. We will authenticate the request and purge all personal records, task data, and associated telemetry from our active databases within thirty (30) business days, sending confirmation once complete.

7. Children's Privacy

TaskFlow is not directed toward children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in certain EU member states), and we do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children. If we discover that personal information has been collected from a child without verified parental consent, we take immediate steps to delete that information from our servers. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please notify us immediately at official.omnicraft@gmail.com.

8. International Data Transfers (GDPR / Cross-Border Transfers)

Your information may be processed and stored on cloud servers located outside of your state, province, or country (primarily within Google Cloud Platform data centers in the United States). By using the Application, you acknowledge and consent to the transfer and storage of your information in jurisdictions that may maintain different data privacy regulations than your domestic jurisdiction. All cross-border transfers are conducted in compliance with legally recognized transfer mechanisms, including Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).

9. Security Measures

We employ administrative, organizational, and technical security safeguards designed to protect personal information from unauthorized access, destruction, loss, alteration, or disclosure. Standard measures include encrypted network transit (HTTPS/TLS), role-based database access permissions, tokenized authorization, and regular codebase vulnerability monitoring. However, no electronic transmission or cloud storage solution is completely secure.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may amend this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect shifts in regulatory mandates, application updates, or industry standards. When changes occur, we will update the "Effective Date" at the top of this document. For material updates affecting data handling rights, we will notify you through an in-app notice or release update log prior to the changes taking effect.
